About Our Team

Two decades behind the lens, still chasing good light.

We are a passionate photographers with over 10 years of experience capturing life's most precious moments. Our journey into photography began with a simple Kodak camera, and has evolved into a professional career where we specialize in portrait, landscape, and event photography.

Our goal is to transform ordinary moments into extraordinary memories through the art of photography. Each shot tells a story, and we strive to capture those stories with authenticity and beauty.

We believe that photography is more than just taking pictures - it's about creating connections, preserving memories, and telling stories through visual art. Our approach combines technical expertise with creative vision to deliver images that resonate deeply with clients and viewers alike.

Based in Lucena City, Quezon, we travel across the province for weddings and events, and shoot portrait sessions from our home studio.

How We Work

Every booking starts with a conversation. We talk through your plans, the look you want, and where the shoot happens - then we build a shot list around it. On the day we arrive early, shoot deliberately, and hand you a gallery you can actually print.

Turnaround is typically 2-3 weeks for full resolution images, with a curated set delivered within 72 hours if you need them sooner.
